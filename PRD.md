# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Vision
Enable merchants to reduce food waste while earning extra revenue, and give consumers affordable meals.

## Goals
- Launch MVP in 1 city within 3 months.
- Reach 5 merchants & 200 active consumers at launch.
- Process ≥100 orders per week by week 6.

## Core Features
- Consumer browse & order (map/list).
- Merchant posting & order management.
- Payment integration (Midtrans).
- QR‑code pickup verification.
- Admin merchant approval.

## Non‑Functional
- 99% uptime, <200 ms page load.
- GDPR‑compliant data handling.
- Support Android 6+ and modern browsers.

## Success Metrics
- Food saved (kg) > 500 in first month.
- Avg order value ≥ Rp30k.
- CSAT ≥ 4.5/5.
