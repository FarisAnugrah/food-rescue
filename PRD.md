# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Vision
Menyelamatkan surplus makanan dari sektor HOREKA (Hotel, Restoran, Katering) dan retail dengan menghubungkan merchant ke konsumen melalui marketplace digital. Terinspirasi dari FoodCycle Indonesia — digitalisasi food rescue dalam skala nasional.

## Problem Statement
- 700 ton limbah makanan dihasilkan sektor HOREKA setiap hari di Indonesia
- Menghasilkan 140.000 m³ gas metana yang menyebabkan perubahan iklim
- 22 juta orang di Indonesia masih hidup dalam kelaparan
- Belum ada platform marketplace yang menghubungkan surplus makanan merchant langsung ke konsumen

## Goals
- Launch MVP in 1 city within 3 months.
- Reach 10 merchants & 200 active consumers at launch.
- Process ≥100 orders per week by week 6.
- Rescue ≥500 kg food in first month.

## Core Features

### MVP (Phase 1)
- Consumer browse & order (map/list view).
- Merchant posting & order management (surprise bag + specific items).
- Payment integration (Midtrans).
- QR-code pickup verification.
- Admin merchant approval & verification.
- Impact counter (kg food saved, CO2 prevented).
- Halal/non-halal labeling.

### Phase 2
- Consumer personal impact dashboard & badges.
- Merchant "Food Waste Warrior" badge system.
- Merchant analytics (revenue, impact, rating).
- Push notifications (pickup reminders, weekly impact summary).
- Reusable container tracking (eco-friendly option).

### Phase 3 (Future)
- Volunteer/rider network untuk distribusi ke komunitas.
- CSR partnership dashboard (brand-sponsored rescue bags).
- Bulk donation mode (merchant → food bank/panti asuhan).
- Food safety certification system.

## Non-Functional
- 99% uptime, <200 ms API response.
- Food safety compliant (pickup window max 4 jam).
- Support Android 6+ and modern browsers.
- Bahasa Indonesia as primary language.
