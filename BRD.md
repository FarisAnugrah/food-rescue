# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## 1. Business Objective
Reduce food waste at merchant level while creating a profitable marketplace platform.

## 2. Target Market
- **Consumer:** Urban, 18-35, price-conscious, eco-aware.
- **Merchant:** Restaurants, bakeries, cafés, supermarkets with daily surplus.

## 3. Revenue Model
| Stream | Model | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Transaction fee | 20-30% commission per order | Primary revenue |
| Merchant subscription | Rp 99-199k/month for premium features | Recurring |
| Promoted listings | Pay-per-click/impression | Secondary |
| CSR partnerships | Brand-sponsored rescue bags | Campaign-based |

## 4. Competitive Landscape
| Competitor | Difference |
|---|---|
| Too Good To Go (Global) | We focus on Indonesian market, local payment, local merchant onboarding |
| Gojek/Grab (promo) | They discount new food; we rescue surplus food specifically |

## 5. Key Risks
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Low merchant adoption | Direct onboarding, show revenue benefit |
| Food safety | Merchant guidelines, user reviews, flagging system |
| Low consumer demand | Marketing via eco-community, campus, social media |

## 6. Success Criteria (Month 3)
- 10+ active merchants
- 500+ registered consumers
- 100+ weekly orders
- 500+ kg food rescued
