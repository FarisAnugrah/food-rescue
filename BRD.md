# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## 1. Business Objective
Mengurangi limbah makanan di level merchant (HOREKA & retail) sambil menciptakan marketplace yang profitable. Platform mendigitalisasi proses food rescue yang saat ini masih manual (via WhatsApp/telepon seperti model FoodCycle Indonesia).

## 2. Market Context
- **700 ton** limbah makanan dari HOREKA setiap hari di Indonesia
- **140.000 m³** gas metana dihasilkan → perubahan iklim
- **22 juta** orang Indonesia masih kelaparan
- Belum ada marketplace digital food rescue di Indonesia

## 3. Target Market
- **Consumer:** Urban, 18-35, price-conscious, eco-aware, tech-savvy.
- **Merchant:** Restaurants, bakeries, cafés, hotels, katering, supermarkets dengan surplus harian.
- **B2B/CSR:** Perusahaan yang ingin sponsori "rescue bags" untuk branding.

## 4. Revenue Model
| Stream | Model | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Transaction fee | 20-30% commission per order | Primary revenue |
| Merchant subscription | Rp 99-199k/month (premium analytics, priority listing) | Recurring |
| Promoted listings | Pay-per-click/impression | Secondary |
| CSR partnerships | Brand-sponsored rescue bags (co-branding) | Campaign-based |
| Bulk donation service | Fee untuk koordinasi donasi ke food bank | Future |

## 5. Value Proposition
| Stakeholder | Value |
|---|---|
| Consumer | Makanan berkualitas 50-70% lebih murah |
| Merchant | Revenue tambahan dari surplus, kurangi biaya buang sampah, CSR image |
| Environment | Kurangi food waste ke TPA, kurangi emisi CO2 |
| Community | Akses makanan terjangkau |

## 6. Competitive Landscape
| Competitor | Model | Our Difference |
|---|---|---|
| Too Good To Go (Global) | Surprise bag marketplace | Fokus pasar Indonesia, payment lokal, onboarding lokal |
| FoodCycle Indonesia | B2B donasi manual (via WA) | Digital marketplace, C2C, scalable, self-service merchant |
| Gojek/Grab promo | Diskon makanan baru | Kita khusus surplus food, impact-driven |

## 7. Key Risks
| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Low merchant adoption | No supply | Direct onboarding, tunjukkan revenue & impact benefit |
| Food safety issues | Trust & legal | Strict pickup window (max 4 jam), halal labeling, merchant guidelines, user reviews |
| Low consumer demand | No revenue | Marketing via eco-community, kampus, social media, impact gamification |
| Regulatory (food safety) | Compliance | Partnership dengan BPOM/dinas kesehatan, food safety guidelines |

## 8. Success Criteria (Month 3)
- 20+ active merchants
- 1,000+ registered consumers
- 200+ weekly orders
- 2,000+ kg food rescued
- ≥5,000 kg CO2 prevented
- Breakeven on operational costs
